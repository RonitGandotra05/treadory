Connect your pedal.

Choose your USB pedal in Chrome’s device picker. This tab stays open while you grant access.

Plug in your pedal, then choose it above.

Pedal missing from the picker? +
  1. Close Treadory’s website USB connection and any other pedal software.
  2. Unplug and reconnect the USB cable. If needed, try a direct USB port.
  3. Click Choose USB pedal again. A VEC Infinity may appear as VEC USB Footpedal.

Chrome blocks keyboard/mouse-only interfaces. This extension requires readable USB pedal input. Website access and USB access are separate permissions.

After connecting, close this tab, open Treadory’s popup on a normal website, allow the site and save your mappings. Release all pedals before using them.
