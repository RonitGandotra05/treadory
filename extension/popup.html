Settings & help +

Actions not working? Close this popup, release and press your pedals on the website, then return here to copy a report. It excludes URLs, page content, custom text and selectors.

Chrome 117+; compatible Edge/Brave desktop browsers may also work. Firefox/Safari lack the required WebHID support. Browser settings, stores, protected pages and some embedded players are excluded. Website shortcuts are simulated and may be ignored; they cannot replace native browser shortcuts. Browser mode does not rewrite hardware outputs or suppress OS mouse clicks. The independent Windows app can replace outputs from one supported, individually identified pedal endpoint. Stop its capture before reconnecting the browser reader. Its settings and recovery controls live in the app.
